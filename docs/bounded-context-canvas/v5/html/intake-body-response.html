Purpose

Capture faithfully what the patient eats and how their body responds, without judging it. Patients log meals (by photo or by hand) and weigh-ins with little effort, even offline; other contexts read these data through queries and never write them.

Ubiquitous Language

  • Diary Entry: a meal record; never deleted
  • Provenance: how an entry was captured (photo or manual)
  • Proposed Estimate: what the AI thinks was eaten; a proposal, never a fact
  • Self Weigh-In: a weight recorded following the protocol
  • Weight Trend: the smoothed series; the daily value is never a headline
  • Plan Fit: how a day's intake sits against the active targets, shown without a verdict

Business Decisions

  • A diary entry is never deleted
  • Provenance and local timestamp are mandatory
  • A photo estimate is stored as a proposal with its confidence
  • Declaring plan adherence carries no penalty
  • Only protocol-compliant weigh-ins smooth the trend
  • Offline entries are synced idempotently
  • AI features run only with AI processing consent

Assumptions

  • – Patients keep logging when declaring costs them almost nothing
  • – A photo estimate is close enough to confirm or adjust in one step
  • – Practitioners trust the trend more than any single day

Verification Metrics

  • – Estimates confirmed without adjustment (%)
  • – Days logged per patient per week
  • – Offline entries synced without duplicates (%)
